Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 20, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect only what is necessary to provide the Service:

  • Account information — your name and email address when you register
  • Expense data — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you enter
  • Usage data — basic log data such as page requests and error events, used solely for debugging

We do not collect payment card details, government IDs, or any sensitive personal identifiers.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the data we collect exclusively to:

  • Provide and maintain your expense tracking account
  • Display your spending history, summaries, and category breakdowns
  • Send transactional emails (e.g., password resets) if you request them
  • Diagnose errors and improve the reliability of the Service

We do not use your financial data to serve targeted advertising, and we never sell your data to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored on servers located in India. We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, all associated expense records are permanently removed within 30 days.

We take reasonable technical measures — including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access controls — to protect your data from unauthorised access.

4. Third-Party Services

Spendly currently relies on the following third-party services in limited ways:

  • Google Fonts — font files are loaded from Google's CDN; this involves a standard DNS lookup from your browser

We do not integrate analytics platforms, advertising networks, or social login providers. If that changes, this policy will be updated before any new integration goes live.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data in your account settings
  • Request deletion of your account and all associated data

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out via the contact information listed on the Service. We aim to respond within 5 business days.
